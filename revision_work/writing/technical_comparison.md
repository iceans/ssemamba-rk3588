# Technical comparison grounded in primary papers

Status: method sections of all six papers were consulted. This table describes architectures, not a numerical benchmark. No training or evaluation of these methods was performed here. SAIST's official web endpoint failed through the browser tool; its public CVF PDF was then retrieved directly and read locally (`evidence/literature/SAIST_CVPR2025.pdf`, pp. 9551–9554).

| Work | Input/task and verified mechanism | Relation to the present revision |
|---|---|---|
| IRMamba, AAAI 2025 | Infrared image mask detection. Parallel convolution/VSS encoding; pixel-difference Mamba uses local differences and spatial information, followed by layer restoration in decoding. [Primary paper, method](https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/AAAI/article/download/33085/35240) | Establishes prior infrared SSM feature design. Our proposed evidence concerns where video features are fused, rather than introducing SSMs to infrared detection. |
| MOCID, AAAI 2025 | Moving infrared target detection. Fourier spatial–temporal attention models motion context; displacement-aware Mamba uses 3D central differences to generate selective parameters. Spatially pooled target/reference features are interleaved and scanned bidirectionally. [Primary paper, method and Fig. 4](https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/AAAI/article/download/33087/35242) | A direct temporal SSM predecessor with interleaving. A claim that interleaving itself is first introduced here is unsupported. |
| ViViM | Medical video object segmentation. A hierarchical temporal Mamba encoder combines spatial and temporal sequences, including forward/backward framewise spatial scans and temporal-first ordering; a decoder produces masks. [Primary paper, §§2.1–2.2](https://arxiv.org/html/2401.14168v3) | Prior video SSM ordering is relevant; its task and segmentation protocol differ from infrared keyframe box detection. |
| SAIST, CVPR 2025 | Infrared image/text mask detection. SR-CLIP forms scene/object descriptions and cross-modal prompts. CG-SAM uses LoRA-adapted image features, prompt-guided decoding and an infrared imaging model to estimate background/target separation. [Primary paper, §§3.1–3.3](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2025/papers/Zhang_SAIST_Segment_Any_Infrared_Small_Target_Model_Guided_by_Contrastive_CVPR_2025_paper.pdf) | A CLIP/SAM adaptation, rather than a video selective-scan model. Compare supervision and output type explicitly. |
| IRSAM, ECCV 2024 | Infrared image segmentation built on Mobile-SAM. Wavelet-based Perona–Malik diffusion augments the encoder; a granularity-aware decoder combines multilevel features and an edge token. [Primary paper, Fig. 2 and §3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2407.07520) | Foundation-model adaptation and mask refinement are distinct from shallow temporal fusion. Do not classify IRSAM as Mamba by its name. |
| LVNet | Multi-frame infrared small target detection using a shallow CNN U structure and deeper video Transformer U structure; multiscale shallow extraction uses parallel 3/5/7 kernels. [Primary paper, method](https://arxiv.org/html/2503.02220v1) | Low-level video information already has prior support. The revision should establish its specific controlled design rather than claim the general shallow-feature principle as new. |

The inference from these sources is to narrow the contribution to the concrete shallow fusion design, its verified scan/reconstruction rule, and reproducible controlled comparisons. None of the sources establishes that this project's SSM, SAAM or interleaving is necessary; that requires this project's own valid ablations.

Suggested insertion location: related work plus a short comparison paragraph at the end of the method overview. Exact section/page numbers remain TODO because the submitted manuscript is missing. Preserve the sources' mask-based metrics; do not place their published scores in a shared AP table without a common box conversion and evaluation protocol.
